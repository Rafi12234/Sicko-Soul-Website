# SICKO SOUL — Landing Page Section Breakdown

Each section listed must have a DISTINCT layout skeleton — no repeating
"image + text" twice in a row.

1. **Preloader** — full black, percentage counter + scramble logo reveal.
2. **Hero** — full-bleed cinematic B&W visual, oversized glitch wordmark,
   marquee tagline strip beneath.
3. **Manifesto** — pinned kinetic-typography paragraph (brand philosophy),
   no imagery, pure black canvas + white text, most "editorial" section.
4. **Lookbook** — horizontal pinned scroll gallery of product/lifestyle
   shots, WebGL hover distortion.
5. **The Streets** — brand story/origin, split asymmetric layout, parallax
   images + scramble pull-quote.
6. **Street Cred** — velocity-reactive marquee of quotes/press
   mentions/"as seen on," dark ticker strip.
7. **Drop Counter** — exclusivity/scarcity section, slot-machine countdown,
   copy reinforcing "not for everyone."
8. **Gallery** — asymmetric masonry of UGC/lifestyle images, grain overlay,
   glitch-on-hover.
9. **Join The Crew** — newsletter capture styled as an "initiation," reactive
   background, magnetic submit button.
10. **Footer** — giant animated outro wordmark, minimal nav, social links,
    copyright in small stencil font.
