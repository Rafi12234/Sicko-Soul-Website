# SICKO SOUL — Brand Bible

## Who this is for
Not everyone. This brand is for people who see themselves as outsiders,
outlaws, self-made, unapologetic — smokers, hustlers, the "spoiled but
dangerous," old money with new attitude, streetwear collectors who want
something that feels illegal to wear. If a customer needs convincing, they
are not the customer.

## Brand Personality
- Cold, confident, a little menacing
- Never explains itself, never apologizes
- Speaks in short, blunt sentences — like a warning, not an invitation
- Feels expensive AND dangerous at the same time (think: tailored suit + gun
  in the pocket, not streetwear-for-teenagers)

## Voice & Tone Examples
BAD (generic streetwear copy): "Check out our new drop — fresh streetwear
for the culture!"

GOOD (Sicko Soul tone):
- "Not made for comfort. Made for consequence."
- "You don't wear this. You survive in it."
- "Limited. On purpose. Most people don't deserve it anyway."
- "SICKO SOUL — for the ones who were never invited."

## Visual Mood
Black and white photography, hard shadows, smoke, chain-link fences, leather,
tattoos, cigarette smoke, muscle cars at night, mugshot-style portraits,
graffiti-tagged concrete, dobermans, brass knuckles, money in monochrome.

## What this brand is NOT
- Not colorful, not playful, not "for everyone"
- Not a friendly customer-service tone
- Not minimalist-Scandinavian clean — it's minimalist-DANGEROUS
- No emojis, no exclamation-point enthusiasm, no "Hey there!"
